**Title:** Fatigue-qualified lightweight composite deck panels for pedestrian and cycle bridges
**Company partner:** Hoyland Composites Ltd (fictional), an SME making fibre-reinforced polymer bridge decks

**Duration:** 24 months (1 August 2024 to 31 July 2026)
**Reference on award:** KTP99999X (fictional)

## Company need

Hoyland's HC-DP400 deck panel sells into footbridge refurbishment, but clients increasingly ask for fatigue evidence that Hoyland cannot produce in-house. The company has no servo-hydraulic test capability and no fatigue design method of its own.

## Project plan

**WP1 (months 1 to 6):** test method. Write a four-point bending fatigue procedure for full-size panels on the University's rig 1. Instrument with eight strain gauges.

**WP2 (months 6 to 18):** test programme. Eight panels (two of the current rev B design, six of a revised rev C), constant amplitude, R = 0.1, to failure or 2 million cycles.

**WP3 (months 15 to 24):** design method. Derive an S-N curve and a damage-based design check. Embed the method in Hoyland's design process.

## Knowledge base contribution

Fatigue testing and analysis of FRP sandwich structures. The University will publish the test method and results in a peer-reviewed journal, subject to the collaboration agreement.

## Intellectual property and data

Hoyland's panel designs, resin formulations and production processes are its background IP and will not be published. Test data generated on the University's rig will be published with the academic paper.

**Does the project raise export control issues?** No. The product is for civil infrastructure.

**Ethics:** not applicable (no human participants, no animals).

## Outputs

- A validated fatigue test procedure (TP-04).
- A test dataset and S-N curve for HC-DP400.
- A journal paper and a conference paper.
