The parties wish to discuss a possible Knowledge Transfer Partnership on fatigue testing of composite deck panels ("the Purpose").

## 2. Confidential information

2.1 Confidential information is any information disclosed by one party to the other for the Purpose that is marked "confidential" or "commercial in confidence", or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

## 3. Obligations

3.1 The receiving party will use confidential information only for the Purpose, and will not disclose it to any third party without the disclosing party's written consent.

3.2 On request, the receiving party will return or destroy the confidential information.

## 4. Duration
