# Research Data Management Policy

**Aire Valley University (fictional)**
**Version 2.0, approved by University Research Committee, 3 June 2025.**

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to research data created or collected by staff, students and visiting researchers, including data from projects with industrial partners.

## 2. Data management plans

2.1 Projects with an external partner must have a data management plan agreed with the partner. Research Contracts must review any plan that commits to sharing data the partner may have rights in.

## 3. Credentials and secrets

3.1 Passwords, access keys, licence keys and other credentials must never be stored in research data files, and must never be deposited.

3.2 Anyone who finds credentials in research data must report them to IT Security, who will arrange for them to be changed.

## 4. Confidential and partner data

4.1 Data that are subject to confidentiality or intellectual property obligations may be deposited only after Research Contracts has confirmed that the relevant agreement allows it.

## 5. Export control

5.1 Research with a potential military or dual-use application, or where a partner has indicated a defence end use, must be referred to the University **Export Control Officer** before any publication, deposit or transfer of data or technology.

5.2 Researchers must not decide for themselves whether export control applies.

## 6. Deposit and access

6.1 Research data must be deposited in **AVU Research Data Online** or a suitable subject or funder repository. AVU Research Data Online replaced the AVU Research Data Archive, which closed to new deposits on 30 September 2025.

6.2 Data that underpin a publication must have a persistent identifier cited in the publication. "Available on request" is not acceptable as the only means of access. Where data cannot be open for legal or contractual reasons, a metadata record must be published and access managed through the Library.

6.3 Deposits larger than 20 GB must be discussed with the Library first.

## 7. Retention

7.1 Research data that underpin a publication must be kept for at least 10 years after publication.

## 8. Contacts

Research Data Service (Library); Research Contracts; Export Control Officer; IT Security. Synthetic contacts.
