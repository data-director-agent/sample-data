# KTP collaboration agreement: extract

> Extract of the clauses relevant to data and publication. Synthetic: the company, university and people are fictional.

**Between:** Aire Valley University (fictional) ("the University") and Hoyland Composites Ltd (fictional) ("the Company")
**Project:** Knowledge Transfer Partnership KTP99999X (fictional)
**Signed:** 25 July 2024. **Commencement date:** 1 August 2024. **Term:** to 31 July 2026. Clauses 4, 6 and 7 survive the end of the term.

## 1. Definitions

**Background IP:** intellectual property owned by a party before the Commencement Date or created outside the Project, including the Company's panel designs, CAD models, layup schedules and resin formulations.

**Company Confidential Information:** the Company's Background IP and any information marked as confidential by the Company.

**Results:** all intellectual property arising from the Project.

**Test Data:** measurements made on the University's test equipment during the Project, including strain, load, displacement, cycle counts and failure modes.

## 4. Intellectual property

4.1 Results vest in the Company.

4.2 The Company grants the University a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual licence to use the Results for academic research and teaching.

## 6. Publication

6.1 The University may publish academic papers about the Project and deposit the Test Data in a data repository, provided that it gives the Company at least **45 days'** written notice, with a copy of the proposed publication.

6.2 Within the notice period, the Company may by written notice:

(a) require the removal of Company Confidential Information; and

(b) require publication to be delayed by up to **90 further days**, where this is needed to file a patent application, stating that reason.

6.3 The Company may not otherwise prevent the publication of the Test Data.

6.4 Nothing in this Agreement requires either party to act in breach of export control law.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential for five years after the end of the term.

7.2 This Agreement supersedes the mutual non-disclosure agreement of 1 March 2024 (reference HC/AVU/2024-03) for information disclosed on or after the Commencement Date.

## Signatures

For the University: Director of Research Contracts. For the Company: Managing Director. (Synthetic.)
