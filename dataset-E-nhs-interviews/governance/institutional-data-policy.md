# Research Data Management Policy

**Don Valley University (fictional)**
**Version 3.2, approved by Research and Innovation Committee, 11 November 2024.**

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to research data created or collected by staff and postgraduate researchers.

## 2. Data management plans

2.1 Postgraduate researchers must have a data management plan approved by their primary supervisor within three months of starting.

## 3. Personal data

3.1 Files containing personal data must be stored on the University secure research drive. File names must not contain participants' names.

3.2 Pseudonymised data (where codes replace names but the data could still identify a person directly or indirectly) remain personal data.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Research data that underpin a thesis or publication must be kept for at least 10 years after the thesis is examined or the publication appears.

4.2 Where participants were told their data would be destroyed sooner, the shorter period applies to their personal data unless the ethics committee approves otherwise.

## 5. Sharing and deposit

5.1 Research data may be shared outside the research team only in line with the consent given and the ethics approval.

5.2 Qualitative data from human participants, including interview transcripts and any data about health, must be reviewed by the **Research Data Steward** before any external deposit. The steward decides, with the PI or supervisor, whether the data may be deposited, and whether access should be open, safeguarded or controlled.

5.3 The UK Data Service is a suitable repository for social science data, including data that need safeguarded or controlled access.

## 6. Contacts

Research Data Steward (Library); Research Ethics Office. Synthetic contacts.
