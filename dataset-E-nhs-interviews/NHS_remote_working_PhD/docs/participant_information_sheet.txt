PARTICIPANT INFORMATION SHEET  (Example University, fictional)   Dated 12/01/2026
Study title: Working from a distance: remote and flexible working and well-being among NHS clinical staff
Researcher: [PhD student], School of Health Services Research, Example University. Funded by a UKRI doctoral studentship.

You are invited to take part in an interview of about 45-60 minutes about your experience of remote, hybrid or flexible working arrangements and how these affect your well-being at work. The interview will be audio-recorded and transcribed by the researcher. Your name and the name of your workplace will be removed from the transcript and replaced with a code. The recordings will be deleted once the transcripts have been checked, and the transcripts will be stored securely on university systems and seen only by the researcher and the supervisory team. Short quotations may be used in the PhD thesis and in academic publications, but you will not be identified. All research data will be destroyed five years after the end of the project. Taking part is voluntary, and you may withdraw up to four weeks after your interview without giving a reason.
