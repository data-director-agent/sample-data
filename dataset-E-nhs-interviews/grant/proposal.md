## Plain English summary

Since the pandemic, more NHS staff work part of their week from home or on flexible patterns. Little is known about how this affects the well-being of clinical staff, whose work is mostly hands-on. I will interview nurses, allied health professionals and clinical managers about their experiences, and use what they tell me to help NHS employers design fairer flexible-working policies.

## Research question

How do NHS clinical staff experience remote, hybrid and flexible working, and how do these arrangements affect their well-being at work?

## Research plan and methods

**Design.** A qualitative interview study.

**Setting and sample.** About **20** clinical staff from **two NHS trusts** in South Yorkshire, sampled purposively by role group and working pattern. I will recruit through trust staff bulletins and Research and Development (R&D) contacts.

**Data collection.** Semi-structured interviews of 45 to 60 minutes, in person or on Microsoft Teams, audio-recorded on an encrypted university device. I will transcribe the interviews myself.

**Analysis.** Reflexive thematic analysis supported by NVivo.
