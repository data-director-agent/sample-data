# Data Management and Access Plan (DAMP)

**Study:** Working from a distance: remote and flexible working and well-being among NHS clinical staff
**Funder:** NIHR Doctoral Fellowship (NIHR999999, fictional)
**Host:** Don Valley University (fictional)
**Template:** Don Valley University PGR DMP (DMPonline)
**Version:** 1.1, 20 Sept 2025, prepared by the fellow. Supervisor sign-off: [pending]

## 1. Data collection

About 20 semi-structured interviews with NHS clinical staff from two trusts. Audio is recorded on an encrypted university Dictaphone and transferred to the university drive on the day of the interview. Data produced:
- audio recordings (MP3), **deleted immediately after transcription**
- transcripts (DOCX, exported to TXT for archiving)
- an interview log (CSV) recording participant code, date, role group, trust and working pattern
- signed consent forms, scanned to PDF.

## 2. Documentation and metadata

Each transcript is named with the participant code only (P01, P02, …). A study-level README will describe the sampling, interview schedule and transcription conventions. The interview log acts as the data dictionary.

## 3. Ethics and legal compliance

**Consent.** Participants will consent to their **anonymised transcripts being archived with the UK Data Service** and reused by other researchers. Consent forms are stored separately from transcripts, and their **filenames use participant codes only**.

**Anonymisation.** Transcripts will be **fully anonymised** before analysis by replacing names and workplaces with codes. Anonymised data are not personal data, so the archived dataset will be openly available.

**Withdrawal.** Participants may withdraw at any time up to the point of archiving.

**Lawful basis.** Public task (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(e)). For any health information disclosed, Art. 9(2)(j) (research).

## 4. Storage and backup

During the project all data are stored on the Don Valley University secure research drive, which is backed up nightly. Access is limited to the fellow and the supervisory team.

## 5. Selection and preservation

Transcripts, the interview log and documentation will be preserved. Audio will not be kept. All research data will be retained for **10 years** after the end of the fellowship, in line with the Don Valley University Research Data Policy.

## 6. Data sharing

At the end of the fellowship the anonymised transcripts and interview log will be deposited with the **UK Data Service** (ReShare) and made available under an open licence.

## 7. Responsibilities and resources

The fellow is responsible for data management, supervised by the primary supervisor. Transcription is done by the fellow, so no extra cost is involved. [Ask the library whether there is a cost for the UK Data Service deposit.]
