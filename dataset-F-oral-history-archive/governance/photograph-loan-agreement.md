## 1. Ownership and copyright

1.1 The lender keeps ownership of the original photographs and any copyright the lender holds in them. Nothing in this agreement transfers copyright to the University.

1.2 The lender confirms that, to the best of their knowledge, they own the photographs or have the right to lend them. The lender is not asked to confirm who took each photograph.

## 2. Use of digital copies

2.1 The University may scan the photographs and use the digital copies for research, teaching, the Mill Voices exhibition at Netherbrook Library, and printed publications arising from the project, with the credit line "Courtesy of [lender]".

2.2 **Online publication** of any image, including on the project website or in an online archive, requires the lender's **prior written consent for each image**.

2.3 The University may keep the digital copies in its archive after the project, subject to clause 2.2.

## 3. Return of originals

3.1 The University will return the originals to the lender **by 31 December 2025**.
