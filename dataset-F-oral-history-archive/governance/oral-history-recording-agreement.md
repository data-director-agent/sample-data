# Mill Voices oral history recording agreement (template, version 2)

> Synthetic test document. Template used for OH01 to OH04. Signed copies are held on paper by the project.

**Project:** Mill Voices, Department of History, Wharfedale University (fictional)

Thank you for taking part in Mill Voices. This agreement sets out how your recording and transcript may be used. Please read it after your interview, choose one access option, and sign.

## 1. Your rights in the recording

1.1 You keep the copyright in your own words. By signing, you give Wharfedale University a **non-exclusive licence** to keep, copy and make your recording and transcript available in the ways you choose below, for education, research, exhibition and archiving.

1.2 You can ask us to change your access choice at any time before the recording is deposited in an archive.

## 2. Access (choose one)

- [ ] **Open.** The recording and transcript may be made publicly available, including online, with my name.
- [ ] **Closed until a date.** The recording and transcript will be kept securely and not made available to anyone outside the project until **25 years** from the date of this agreement. After that date, they may be made available as in "Open".
- [ ] **Anonymous.** My name and identifying details will be removed before the recording or transcript is made available.

## 3. Other people mentioned

3.1 The University may remove or restrict parts of the recording that mention other people, if publishing them could cause harm or distress.

Once signed, this agreement continues after the interviewee's death. If the agreement has not been signed, the University will not make the recording available outside the project.
