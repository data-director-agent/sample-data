# Dataset F: Mill Voices oral histories and photographs

Arts and humanities (oral history, local and industrial history). Twenty files: transcripts, TEI XML, CSV, PNG images, XLSX, DOCX, text and some leftovers. All data, people, places and organisations are synthetic.

- `MillVoices_handover/`: the dataset folder.
- `grant/`: the grant proposal (`proposal.md`), award letter (`award-letter.md`) and data management plan (`dmp.md`).
- `governance/`: the institution's research data policy, the oral history recording agreement template and the photograph loan agreement template.

## Context

- **Researcher:** the principal investigator of a two-year community heritage project, whose research associate has just left. The folder holds four interview transcripts, digitised photographs lent by residents, new photographs of the mill buildings, and exhibition text (about 2.5 MB).
- **Funder:** AHRC (UK Research and Innovation). A final report, saying where the data can be found, is due at the end of December 2026.
- **Institution:** Wharfedale University (fictional), with its own repository and policy. A local history society is a project partner.
- **Plan:** deposit the archive before the final report.
- **Budget for deposit fees:** none.

The researchers, town, mills, local history society and project are fictional. The funder and archives are real.
