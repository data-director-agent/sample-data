## 1. Excellence (extract)

### 1.1 Quality and pertinence of the project's research and innovation objectives

FLOWNET-DN trains ten doctoral candidates in lattice-Boltzmann (LBM) and related mesoscopic methods for flows around bluff bodies, from vortex shedding behind cylinders to wind loading on structures. The network will release a shared, open-source LBM toolkit and a benchmark database of validated cases.

### 1.3 Quality of the supervision and training (extract)

Each DC has a supervisor and co-supervisor from different beneficiaries, and at least one secondment. All DCs take network-wide training in open science, FAIR data and research software engineering (Month 9 school, Valmont).

## DC7 individual project (Northmoor)

**Title:** Grid convergence and wake statistics for 2D cylinder flow at low Reynolds number
**Objectives:** (i) Implement a D2Q9 BGK solver in the FLOWNET toolkit. (ii) Run a parameter study at Re 20 to 120 on two grid resolutions (12 cases) on the Northmoor HPC cluster. (iii) Extract drag, lift and Strouhal number and compare with the literature. (iv) Release all code and data as a FLOWNET benchmark case.
**Expected results:** A validated benchmark with open code, input files and results.
**Planned secondment:** Technische Hochschule Albrechtsau, 3 months (code review and GPU porting).
**Enrolment:** PhD, Northmoor Institute of Technology.
