# Research Data and Computing Storage Policy

**Northmoor Institute of Technology (fictional)**
**Version 1.4, approved by Research and Innovation Committee, 30 January 2024.**

## 1. Scope

This policy covers research data and research software created by staff and research students, and the use of the Institute's research computing storage.

## 2. Research computing storage

2.1 **Scratch storage** (`/mnt/parscratch`) is for temporary working files during computations. It is **not backed up**.

2.2 Files on scratch storage that have not been accessed for **60 days** may be deleted automatically, without notice.

2.3 When a user leaves the Institute, their computing account is closed **90 days** after their leaving date. Any files remaining on scratch or home storage are then deleted. Supervisors are responsible for arranging the transfer of any data that must be kept before the account closes.

2.4 Project storage is backed up nightly. It is the correct place for data that must be kept beyond a computation.

## 3. Retention

3.1 Research data and software that underpin a thesis or a publication must be kept for at least 10 years after the thesis is examined or the publication appears.

3.2 Where the full data are too large to keep, researchers must keep the inputs, code and reduced data needed to regenerate them, and record what was not kept and why.

## 4. Research software

4.1 Research software should be released under an open-source licence where the Institute owns the rights and no third-party terms prevent it.

4.2 Where software includes third-party code, the licence of that code must be respected. The Research Software Engineering (RSE) team advises on licence compatibility and approves the licence for any software release.

## 5. Third-party materials

5.1 Commercial software, models and input files licensed to the Institute must not be shared outside the Institute unless the licence allows it.
