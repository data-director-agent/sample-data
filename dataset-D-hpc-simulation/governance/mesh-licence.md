# MeshForge Commercial Edition: end-user licence (extract)

> Extract of the clauses relevant to research use and sharing. Synthetic: the software vendor, product and licensee are fictional.

**Product:** MeshForge Commercial Edition 7.1
**Licensee:** Department of Mechanical Engineering, Northmoor Institute of Technology (fictional)
**Licence type:** academic site licence, annual. **Current term:** 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2025.

## 1. Definitions

**Software:** the MeshForge program and its documentation.

**Output Files:** mesh and geometry files generated with the Software, in any format, including files converted to other formats.

**Results:** quantities computed by third-party solvers using Output Files as input, such as flow fields, forces and coefficients.

## 2. Grant of licence

2.1 The Licensee may use the Software, and Output Files, for internal research and teaching only.

## 3. Restrictions

3.1 The Licensee must not distribute, publish, sublicense or deposit in any repository the Software or any Output Files, in whole or in part, without the vendor's prior written consent.

3.2 Each Output File carries a licence header that must not be removed.

## 4. Results

4.1 Results are not Output Files. The Licensee may publish Results, provided that the Output Files themselves are not distributed.

4.2 Publications using Output Files should acknowledge the use of MeshForge.

## 5. Term and termination

5.1 On expiry or termination, the Licensee must stop using the Software. Output Files created during the term may be kept for internal archival purposes only.
