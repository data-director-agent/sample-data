# Research Data Management Policy

**University of the Pennine Vale (fictional)**
**Version 2.1, approved by Research Committee, 9 March 2023.** Next review: March 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all research data created or collected by staff and students of the University, whatever the source of funding.

## 2. Responsibilities

2.1 The principal investigator (PI) is responsible for the management of research data in their project.

2.2 When a member of staff who manages research data leaves the University, the PI must name a successor data custodian and record the location of the data **before the staff member's last working day**. The custodian is recorded in the University research information system.

## 3. Data management plans

3.1 Every funded project must have a data management plan that meets the funder's requirements. Where a funder asks for a plan to be agreed with a third party (for example a data centre), the PI is responsible for doing so.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Research data that underpin published results must be kept for at least **10 years** from the date of publication, or longer if the funder requires it.

## 5. Deposit and access

5.1 Research data that underpin published results must be deposited in a suitable repository by the time of publication, unless there are legal, ethical or commercial reasons not to.

5.2 Deposit with a funder's designated data centre or another trusted discipline repository satisfies this policy. The University repository is used where no suitable external repository exists.

5.3 A metadata record for every deposited dataset, including those held externally, must be created in the University research information system, with a link to the dataset's DOI.

5.4 Where data are subject to restrictions from landowners, partners, licensors or participants, the restrictions must be recorded and respected. The Research Office will advise.

## 6. Licences

6.1 Openly shared data should normally be licensed under CC BY 4.0, unless the repository or funder requires otherwise.
