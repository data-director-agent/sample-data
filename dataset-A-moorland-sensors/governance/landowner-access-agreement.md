# Access agreement for research monitoring equipment

> Transcribed from a signed paper copy. Synthetic: the landowner, farm and university are fictional.

**Between:** University of the Pennine Vale (fictional) ("the University"), represented by Prof. A. Example
**and:** Mr R. Thackeray, Upper Hallam Farm ("the Landowner")
**Project:** MICROMOOR, NERC grant NE/X999999/1 (fictional)
**Date signed:** 26 February 2024
**Term:** from 1 March 2024 to **31 March 2026**, unless extended in writing.

## 1. Permitted activity

1.1 The Landowner permits the University to install, maintain and remove **three** solar-powered environmental monitoring nodes at the locations shown in Schedule 1, and to visit them for maintenance and data download.

1.2 Any additional equipment, or any change of location, requires the Landowner's prior written agreement.

## 2. Access

2.1 Access is by the farm track from Moorside Lane only. Visitors must close all gates and keep dogs on leads.

2.2 The University will give the Landowner at least 24 hours' notice of visits during the lambing period (1 April to 15 May).

## 3. Data

3.1 Environmental measurements collected by the equipment belong to the University.

3.2 The University may use and publish the measurements for research.

## 4. Removal and reinstatement

4.1 At the end of the term the University will remove all equipment and reinstate the ground within 30 days.

## 5. Termination

5.1 Either party may end this agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.
