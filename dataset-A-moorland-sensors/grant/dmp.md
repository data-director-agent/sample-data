# Outline Data Management Plan (NERC)

**Project title:** Microclimate gradients and soil moisture on a restored upland moor (MICROMOOR)
**Principal Investigator / Grant holder:** Prof. A. Example, University of the Pennine Vale (fictional)
**Version:** 1.0, submitted with the proposal (June 2023)

The lead PI is responsible for the data from all components of the project.

## Will the grant produce data?

Yes.

## Datasets to be produced

| Dataset | Description | Format | Est. volume | Data centre | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MICROMOOR logger data | 30-minute air temperature, RH, soil temperature, soil moisture (volumetric water content, %) and PM2.5 from three nodes (N01 to N03), 12 months | CSV | about 5 MB | NERC EDS (EIDC) | End of award |
| Site metadata | Node coordinates (GPS) and elevations, sensor models, installation dates | CSV | < 1 MB | NERC EDS (EIDC) | End of award |
| Calibration records | Pre-deployment ice-bath offsets for each sensor | XLSX | < 1 MB | NERC EDS (EIDC) | End of award |

## Existing or third-party data

None. All data will be generated by the project.

## Data management notes

- **Quality control:** Data will be quality-controlled by the PDRA. Calibration offsets will be applied before deposit, so the deposited files are calibrated values.
- **Personal or sensitive data:** None. The project collects environmental measurements only. Node locations will be published in full to support reuse and site revisits.
- **Embargo:** We may request a short period of exclusive use while the main paper is prepared, then make the data openly available.
- **Deposit:** At the end of the award we will offer a copy of all datasets to the appropriate NERC Environmental Data Centre (expected to be the EIDC), and will work with the data centre on the full DMP.
- **Responsibility for data management:** Dr D. Whitcombe (PDRA).
- **Costs:** TBC with the data centre.
