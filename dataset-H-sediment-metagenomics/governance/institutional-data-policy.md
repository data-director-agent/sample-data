# Research Data Management Policy

**Holmebridge University (fictional)**
**Version 2.1, approved by University Research Committee, 9 May 2025.**

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to research data created or collected by staff and postgraduate researchers.

## 2. Storage

2.1 Research data must be stored on the University's backed-up Research Data Storage (RDS) or another approved service. HPC scratch areas are not backed up and are purged automatically; they must not be the only copy of any data.

## 3. Sensitive data

3.1 Data from which a living person could be identified are personal data and must be handled in line with data protection law.

3.2 Human DNA sequence data may be personal data even when obtained incidentally, for example from environmental or microbial samples. Researchers must consult the **Research Data Service** before depositing any data that contain human sequence reads. The Research Data Service, with the Data Protection Officer where needed, advises whether such reads should be removed before deposit.

## 4. Genetic resources

4.1 Researchers using genetic resources from outside the UK must keep records showing that the resources were accessed legally, including any permits, prior informed consent and mutually agreed terms from the provider country. The **Research Contracts Office** advises on access and benefit-sharing and holds the University's due diligence records.

4.2 Material transfer agreements must be signed by the Research Contracts Office **before** material is received.

## 5. Sharing and deposit

5.1 Raw sequence data should be deposited in an International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC) archive, such as the European Nucleotide Archive. This satisfies the University's deposit requirement for those data.

5.2 Other data underpinning publications must be deposited in the Holmebridge Research Data Repository or another suitable repository that issues a persistent identifier.

5.3 A metadata record for every dataset, including those deposited externally, must be created in the University's research information system.
