## Vision

Wholegrain cereals are linked to better metabolic health, but how barley fibre reshapes the gut microbiome in healthy adults is poorly understood. GutGrain will run a 12-week parallel-group dietary intervention (wholegrain barley versus refined grain) and follow changes in microbial genes and short-chain fatty acids, producing an open, well-described dataset for the diet and microbiome community.

## Approach

**WP1: Recruitment and intervention (months 6 to 18).** 60 healthy adults (18 to 65) from the local community, randomised to wholegrain barley (WG) or refined grain (RG) study foods. Stool samples at weeks 0, 2, 4, 8 and 12, and a follow-up at week 16. Food diaries and health questionnaires.

**WP2: Laboratory analysis (months 10 to 30).** Shotgun metagenomic sequencing (paired-end 150 bp) at the Nidderdale Genomics Facility. Targeted short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) measurement by GC-FID.

**WP3: Integration and synthesis (months 24 to 36).** Taxonomic and functional profiling; links between diet, microbial genes and SCFA.

**Risks.** Drop-out (mitigation: over-recruit by 10 % if needed). Sample degradation (mitigation: home collection kits with stabiliser, freezing within 24 hours).
