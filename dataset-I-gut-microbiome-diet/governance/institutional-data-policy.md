# Research Data Management Policy

**Nidderdale University (fictional)**
**Version 1.3, approved by University Research Committee, 14 January 2026.**

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to research data created or collected by staff and postgraduate researchers.

## 2. Storage

2.1 Research data must be stored on the University's backed-up Research Data Store (RDS) or another approved service.

## 3. Personal data

3.1 Data from which a living person could be identified are personal data and must be handled in line with data protection law. Data about health are special category data.

3.2 Keys that link study codes to participants' identities must be stored separately from the research data, in the restricted Secure Data Area, and be accessible only to named staff. They must never be stored in shared project folders.

3.3 Human DNA sequence data may be personal data, including human reads in sequence data from microbiome samples taken from people.

3.4 Data from human participants may be shared only in ways consistent with the participants' consent and the ethics approval. Researchers must consult the **Research Data Service** and, where needed, the **Data Protection Officer** before sharing such data. Changes to how data will be shared need an amendment approved by the Faculty Research Ethics Committee.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Research data underpinning publications must be retained for at least ten years after the end of the project, or longer if the funder requires it.

## 5. Sharing and deposit

5.1 Data must be shared through a recognised repository that issues a persistent identifier. A project website is not an acceptable means of preserving or sharing research data on its own.

5.2 Sequence data should be deposited in an International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC) archive, such as the European Nucleotide Archive. Where sequence data from human participants cannot be shared openly, a controlled-access archive, such as the European Genome-phenome Archive, should be used.

5.3 Other data may be deposited in the Nidderdale Research Data Repository, which offers open and controlled access and preserves data for at least ten years at no charge to University researchers.

## 6. Costs

6.1 Data management costs may be charged to a grant only where the funder's terms allow and the costs are incurred within the grant period.

## 7. Contacts

Research Data Service (Library); Data Protection Officer; Faculty Research Ethics Committee; Research Office. Synthetic contacts.
