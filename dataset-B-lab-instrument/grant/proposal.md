# EPSRC New Investigator Award: proposal text

**Title:** Manganese doping for air-stable halide perovskite emitters
**Applicant:** Dr S. Haldane, Department of Physics & Astronomy, Calderbrook University (fictional)

**Award reference (on award):** EP/X999999/1 (fictional)
**Project partner:** Acme Perovskite Ltd (fictional). Letter of support attached.

## Vision

Caesium lead bromide (CsPbBr₃) films emit bright, narrow green light, but they degrade within weeks in ambient air. Small amounts of manganese may stabilise the lattice. Nobody has mapped how stability depends on Mn content in thin films made from commercially relevant precursors. This award will do that, and will build my independent group.

## Approach

**Objective 1.** Make a composition series of Mn-doped CsPbBr₃ films (0 to 15 at% Mn) by spin-coating. Precursor batches come both from our lab and from Acme Perovskite Ltd, so that results transfer to scaled-up material.

**Objective 2.** Characterise structure and morphology by powder X-ray diffraction (XRD, Cu Kα) and scanning electron microscopy (SEM). Measure composition by EDX on the department's SEM.

**Objective 3.** Age films in controlled and ambient conditions. Repeat XRD at 30 and 90 days to track secondary phases.

**Objective 4.** Correlate structural stability with photoluminescence (PL), via a planned collaboration with a spectroscopy group (TBC).

A PhD student, funded by the department, will carry out most of the synthesis and characterisation from year 1.

## Applicant and team capability

I have three years of postdoctoral experience in perovskite thin-film growth. The department provides an X-ray diffractometer and SEM. Acme Perovskite Ltd will provide precursor material and in-kind time from their process chemist.
