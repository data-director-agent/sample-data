# Research Data Policy

**Calderbrook University (fictional)**
**Version 3.0, approved by Senate, 17 April 2024.** Replaces version 2.0 (2022) and version 1.2 (2021). Earlier versions are withdrawn and their web pages have been removed.
**Published at:** <https://www.example.ac.uk/research/data-policy>

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to all research data created at or on behalf of Calderbrook University.

## 2. Data management plans

2.1 Every externally funded project must have a data management plan (DMP), whether or not the funder asks for one.

2.2 The DMP must be **reviewed by the Research Data Service within six months of the project start date**, and updated when the project changes materially.

## 3. Ownership and agreements

3.1 Unless an agreement says otherwise, research data created by staff and students belong to the University.

3.2 Where a collaboration, licence or consultancy agreement places conditions on data or results, those conditions take precedence over the sharing expectations in this policy. Researchers must consult the Research Office (Contracts) before sharing any data covered by such an agreement.

## 4. Personal data

4.1 Personal data, including the names and contact details of staff recorded in instrument files, must not be included in openly shared datasets unless the people concerned have agreed. Where agreement has not been recorded, the personal data must be removed before deposit.

## 5. Retention and preservation

5.1 Research data that underpin publications must be preserved for at least 10 years from the date any privileged access period expires or, if others have accessed the data, from the last date on which access was requested.

## 6. Deposit and access

6.1 Research data that underpin publications must be deposited in the Calderbrook Research Data Repository, or in a suitable external repository, no later than the date of publication.

6.2 Access may be delayed (embargoed) for a stated period to allow a patent application or to meet the terms of an agreement. The Research Office must approve any embargo for commercial reasons.

6.3 Data should be shared in open, non-proprietary formats where possible. Proprietary instrument files may be deposited alongside open exports.
