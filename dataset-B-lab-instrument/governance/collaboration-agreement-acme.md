# Collaboration agreement: extract

> Extract of the clauses relevant to data and publication. Synthetic: the partner, university and people are fictional.

**Between:** Calderbrook University (fictional) ("the University") and Acme Perovskite Ltd (fictional) ("Acme")
**Project:** Manganese doping for air-stable halide perovskite emitters, EPSRC grant EP/X999999/1 (fictional)
**Effective date:** 1 October 2024. **Term:** to the end of the grant.
**University contact:** Dr S. Haldane. **Acme contact:** Process Chemistry Manager.

## 1. Definitions

**Partner Materials:** precursor materials supplied by Acme, and information about their composition and preparation.

**Partner-derived Results:** results, including data, obtained from samples made with Partner Materials.

**Confidential Information:** the composition and preparation of the Partner Materials, and any other information marked as confidential by either party.

## 4. Partner Materials

4.1 Acme will supply precursor batches at no cost. Each batch will be identified by a batch code recorded by the University.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 The University will not disclose Acme's Confidential Information to any third party, or publish it, without Acme's written consent.

## 8. Publication and deposit of results

8.1 Before submitting for publication, presenting or depositing in any repository any Partner-derived Results, the University will send Acme a written **Notice of Proposed Publication** with a copy of the proposed material.

8.2 Acme may, within **90 days** of receiving the notice, require the removal of its Confidential Information, or require a delay under clause 8.3.

8.3 If Acme reasonably believes the material contains patentable subject matter, it may require the University to delay publication or deposit for **up to a further six months** from the end of the 90-day period, so that a patent application can be filed.

8.4 If Acme does not respond within the 90 days, the University may proceed.

8.5 Nothing in this agreement restricts the University from publishing or depositing results that are not Partner-derived Results.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 Intellectual property in the Partner Materials remains with Acme.

9.2 Intellectual property in results arising from the project belongs to the University. Acme will have the first option to negotiate a licence to Partner-derived Results.

Signed for the University: Director of Research Services. Signed for Acme: Managing Director.
