SYCAWS - South Yorkshire Community Activity & Wellbeing Survey (wave 1) - question wording
Q1  How old are you?
Q2  How would you describe your gender?
Q3  What is your ethnic group?
Q4  What is the first part of your postcode (e.g. S10)?
Q5  What is your job (or main activity if not working)?
Q6  In which sector do you work?
Q7  In a typical week, about how many minutes of moderate physical activity (e.g. brisk walking) do you do?
Q8  On average, how many hours do you sleep per night?
Q9  Do you have a long-standing health condition?
Q10 If yes, please describe it.
WB  [7 statements about feeling good and functioning well, each answered 1-5; total supplied]
Q11 Is there anything else you would like to tell us?
Q12 May we contact you about future studies? If yes, please give your email address.
