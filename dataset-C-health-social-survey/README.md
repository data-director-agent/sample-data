## Welcome

This folder holds the first wave of an online survey about physical activity and wellbeing, as left by the researcher who ran it. It is a small, tidy-looking social-science dataset: one main table of responses, the survey documents, and a few copies of files made along the way. It suits anyone wanting to practise preparing survey data for sharing.

## Research background

The South Yorkshire Community Activity and Wellbeing Survey (SYCAWS) asks how everyday physical activity relates to wellbeing among adults in South Yorkshire, and whether the relationship differs by sector of employment, long-term health conditions and neighbourhood. Wave 1 was an online questionnaire of about ten minutes, open for several weeks in early 2025 and answered by around 300 adults recruited through community groups, GP surgeries, local newsletters and social media. It covered demographics, postcode district, occupation, weekly activity minutes, sleep, long-term health conditions, a seven-item wellbeing scale and a free-text comment box. Respondents could also leave an email address if they were willing to be contacted for a second wave.
