# Research Data Policy

**Lindenford University (fictional)**
**Version 4, approved by University Research Committee, 5 June 2024.**

## 1. Scope

This policy covers research data created or collected by University staff and students.

## 2. Responsibilities

2.1 Principal investigators are responsible for managing research data in line with funder requirements, this policy and the conditions of their ethics approval.

2.2 The Research Data Steward in the Library advises on data management plans, anonymisation and deposit.

## 3. Retention

3.1 Research data that underpin published results must be retained for at least **10 years after the end of the project**, or longer where the funder requires it.

3.2 Personal data must not be kept for longer than participants were told, unless they have been told otherwise and the ethics committee has approved the change.

## 4. Data from human participants

4.1 Data from human participants may be shared outside the research team only where the consent and the participant information allow it, or where the ethics committee has approved the sharing.

4.2 Before any external deposit of data from human participants, the dataset must be reviewed by the Research Data Steward for disclosure risk. The steward may require further anonymisation, controlled access, or that the data are not deposited.

## 5. Deposit

5.1 Data should be deposited in a trusted repository appropriate to the discipline. The UK Data Service (including ReShare) is the preferred repository for social science data. The University repository may be used where no discipline repository is suitable.
