# ESRC Data Management Plan

**Project:** South Yorkshire Community Activity and Wellbeing Survey (SYCAWS)
**PI:** Dr Jess Whitlock, Lindenford University (fictional)
**Submitted with the application, July 2024.** Not updated since award.

## 1. Assessment of existing data

We reviewed existing UK surveys of physical activity and wellbeing available through the UK Data Service. None provides postcode-district coverage of South Yorkshire with the activity measures and open comments we need, so new data collection is justified. We will use published area classifications for context but will not link them to individual responses.

## 2. Information on new data

One quantitative dataset: about 300 responses to a wave 1 online survey, collected from February to March 2025 on Qualtrics. It will be exported as CSV and analysed in R and SPSS. Open-comment responses will be held in the same file. A codebook giving every variable, question wording and response code will be produced during data collection and kept with the data.

## 3. Quality assurance of data

Qualtrics validation will restrict numeric fields to plausible ranges (for example, age 18 to 110). Responses completed in under 90 seconds will be flagged as possible "speeders". Data cleaning will be scripted in R and documented in a cleaning log, and the raw export will be kept unaltered.

## 4. Backup and security of data

Data will be stored on the Lindenford University secure research drive, which is backed up nightly and accessible only to the named research team. Laptops will be encrypted. Contact emails for wave 2 will be held in a separate password-protected file.

## 5. Expected difficulties in data sharing

None anticipated. The survey is anonymous and does not collect names or addresses.

## 6. Consent, anonymisation and strategies to enable data sharing

The participant consent statement will include consent for anonymised data to be archived with the UK Data Service and shared with other researchers. Before deposit, data will be anonymised by removing names and contact details. No other directly identifying information is collected.

## 7. Copyright and intellectual property rights

Copyright in the dataset belongs to Lindenford University. The questionnaire and wellbeing scale were developed by the team.

## 8. Responsibilities

The PI is responsible for data management. The RA will carry out data entry, cleaning and anonymisation, and will prepare the data for archiving. The Co-I will review anonymisation.

## 9. Preparation of data for sharing and archiving

At the end of the project the anonymised dataset, codebook, questionnaire and cleaning log will be deposited with the **UK Data Service ReShare** repository for open reuse by researchers, under its standard end-user licence. Data will be retained for **10 years** after the end of the project in line with Lindenford University's Research Data Policy.
