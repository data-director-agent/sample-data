## 1. Aims and objectives

SYCAWS asks how everyday physical activity relates to wellbeing among working-age and older adults in South Yorkshire, and whether that relationship differs by sector of employment, long-term health conditions and neighbourhood. The objectives are to:

1. run a wave 1 online survey of about **300 adults** in South Yorkshire (spring 2025)
2. describe activity and wellbeing patterns by age, gender, ethnicity, sector and postcode district
3. design a wave 2 follow-up with consenting respondents (subject to further funding)
4. produce a reusable, documented, anonymised dataset for secondary analysis.

## 3. Methods

**Sampling and recruitment.** Convenience sample recruited through community groups, GP surgery noticeboards, local authority newsletters and social media. Adults aged 18 or over living in South Yorkshire are eligible.

**Instrument.** A 10-minute online questionnaire (Qualtrics), including demographics, postcode district, occupation, employment sector, weekly activity minutes, sleep, long-standing health conditions, a seven-item wellbeing scale and an open comment box. Respondents may leave an email address for follow-up.

**Fieldwork.** The survey is open for **eight weeks from early February 2025** and closes at the end of March.

**Analysis.** Descriptive statistics and multivariable regression of wellbeing score on activity, adjusting for demographic and health covariates. Open comments will be analysed thematically.

## 4. Ethics

Ethical approval will be sought from the Lindenford University Research Ethics Committee before fieldwork begins. Participants will see an information sheet and an on-screen consent statement before the first question. The survey is anonymous apart from the optional email address, which will be stored separately.
